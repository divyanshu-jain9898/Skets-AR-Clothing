My Office - Diwali Edition
==========================

Save a photo of your office here as  my-office.jpg  (my-office.png or
my-office.webp also work) and rebuild. The app turns it into a Diwali canvas
painting (diyas, lanterns, lights and fireworks in the windows) and shows it
under Backgrounds > My Office - Diwali Edition for everyone.

A landscape photo taken at eye level, with the room's main features in the
middle, works best. People can also paint their own office in the app:
Backgrounds > Paint my office from a photo (kept only on their device).
